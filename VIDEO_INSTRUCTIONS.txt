========================================================================
HOW TO UPDATE YOUR VIDEO INTRODUCTION EMBED LINK
========================================================================

When your personal Video Introduction (Video CV) is ready and uploaded 
to YouTube as an Unlisted video, follow these quick steps:

STEP 1: GET YOUR YOUTUBE VIDEO ID
----------------------------------
From your YouTube video link, copy the 11-character video ID.
Examples:
- If link is: https://youtu.be/abc123XYZ45
  -> Your ID is: abc123XYZ45
- If link is: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=abc123XYZ45
  -> Your ID is: abc123XYZ45


STEP 2: OPEN index.html
------------------------
Open "index.html" in VS Code, Notepad, or any code editor.
Search for: "videoEmbedFrame" (around line 180).

You will see:
<iframe 
  id="videoEmbedFrame"
  src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/dQw4w9WgXcQ?rel=0" 
  title="Yeareed Mahmud - Video Introduction" 
  ...


STEP 3: REPLACE THE ID
-----------------------
Replace "dQw4w9WgXcQ" with your own 11-character video ID.
For example:
  src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/abc123XYZ45?rel=0"


STEP 4: SAVE & TEST
--------------------
Save the file and refresh your browser. Your unlisted video will now 
play seamlessly in the portfolio!
